Five Ways to Build Faith at the Dinner Table

By Family Ministries · Oct 12, 2026 · 4 min read

Faith is often passed down not in big moments, but in small, repeated ones. The dinner table is one of the best places to start.

Share one blessing from the day. Pray for one neighbor by name. Read one verse aloud and ask the youngest what it means.

These moments may feel small, but over years they become the foundation of a lasting faith.

"Start children off on the way they should go, and even when they are old they will not turn from it.". Proverbs 22:6
